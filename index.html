Welcome to Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is a beautiful island country in South Asia. It is famous for its golden beaches, green mountains, ancient cities, wildlife and rich cultural heritage. Tourists from all around the world visit Sri Lanka to experience its natural beauty and friendly people.

Famous Places to Visit

1. Sigiriya Rock Fortress

Sigiriya is one of Sri Lanka's most famous historical attractions. It is known for its ancient rock fortress, beautiful gardens and impressive views.

Location: Central Province

2. Ella

Ella is a beautiful mountain destination surrounded by tea plantations, waterfalls and green hills. It is popular among nature lovers and hikers.

Location: Uva Province

3. Galle Fort

Galle Fort is a historic coastal attraction known for its colonial architecture, narrow streets and ocean views.

Location: Southern Province

4. Mirissa Beach

Mirissa is famous for its sandy beaches, beautiful sunsets and opportunities for whale watching.

Location: Southern Province

About Sri Lanka

Commercial Capital: Colombo

Currency: Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR)

Official Languages: Sinhala and Tamil
